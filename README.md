## 📌 Project Overview

This project is part of the Digital Literacy course, where the objective was to take on the role of a Student Digital Ambassador and understand how to use digital tools effectively, build a basic online presence, and practice safe and responsible online behavior.

As part of the project, I completed a series of tasks that focused on practical aspects such as creating an infographic, setting up professional profiles, exploring coding and collaboration platforms, writing formal emails, and studying cybercrime awareness. Overall, the project was more focused on real-world application rather than just theoretical understanding.

---

## 📂 Tasks & What I Did

### Task 1 – Infographic

I created a simple infographic using Canva to explain the concept of digital literacy. It includes topics such as basic digital tools, online safety, and email etiquette. The aim was to keep the design clear and easy to understand.

---

### Task 2 – Digital Portfolio

In this task, I set up my profiles on platforms like GitHub and LinkedIn. This helped me understand how these platforms are used to build a professional presence and how they can be useful in the future.

---

### Task 3 – Platforms

For this part, I completed a beginner-level problem on HackerRank to get familiar with coding practice platforms.
Additionally, I created a Google Form quiz and explored how responses are collected and managed, which gave me insight into collaboration tools.

---

### Task 4 – Email & Etiquette

I wrote two formal emails — one requesting an assignment extension and another expressing interest in an internship.
I also created a checklist covering basic do’s and don’ts for responsible social media use.

---

### Task 5 – Cybercrime Awareness

In this task, I worked on a case study related to UPI fraud and created a prevention checklist. This helped me understand how common online scams work and the importance of being cautious while using digital platforms.
