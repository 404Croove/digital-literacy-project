## Stay Safe Online

- Never enter your UPI PIN to receive money  
- Don’t scan QR codes from unknown people  
- Always check payment requests carefully  
- Never share OTPs or bank details  
- Be cautious of urgent or “too good” offers  
- Use strong, unique passwords  
- Enable 2FA where possible  
- Verify websites before entering details
